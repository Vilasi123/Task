Enhancing Quality through Collaboration:

Transforming Healthcar Together

11th, 12th-13th April, 2025 Hotel Pullman Aerocity New Delhi

CAHOCON 2025 is set to be a landmark international conclave, hosted by the Consortium of Accredited Healthcare Organizations (CAHO) and endorsed by respected bodies such as ISQua, ASQua, and QCI. Themed "Enhancing Quality through Collaboration: Transforming Healthcare Together", this year's event will spotlight the pivotal role of collaboration in driving progress across the healthcare landscape. The tagline, "Collaboration: The heartbeat of progress", encapsulates the spirit of the event.


Taking place on the 12th and 13th of April 2025 at the luxurious Hotel Pullman, Aerocity, New Delhi, the event will bring together distinguished healthcare leaders, professionals, researchers, and educators.
